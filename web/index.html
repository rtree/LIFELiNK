HOW IT WORKS

Five steps to connect your people.

Set up first: a local SIM, carrier three-way calling, Google sign-in, World App, and default phone-app permission are required for SOSV2. Carrier and device behavior may vary.

1

Press the SOS button

Use three quick taps or a two-second hold in the app, or long-press a linked +Beacon. With monitoring configured, the Beacon can trigger while your phone is locked.

2

Share location context

Available coarse location context and its freshness help your contact understand where you are. Precise coordinates and street addresses are not stored.

3

AI joins your contact

Your phone calls the AI and your registered contact, then asks your carrier to merge the calls. The AI shares available context and can listen to the conversation.

Example introduction: “This is LIFELiNK's AI assistant. Your registered contact has triggered an SOS. I can share the available location context and notes.”
4

Real-time info is shared

Coarse location, event updates and call transcripts appear in the app and are relayed to participating Discord friends. Call audio files are not stored.

5

Your people can act

Consenting family and friends receive Discord DMs and can reply with useful context for the AI to relay. They must complete setup and share a server with the bot first.

FOR YOUR PEOPLE

They just need Discord.

No new app. No account setup during a crisis. The people you trust get the alert where they already are — with everything they need to help in one message.

  • Coarse location context, not precise coordinates
  • Call transcripts relayed as Discord messages
  • Replies passed to the AI during an active call
  • Your status, including battery level
  • Invite, consent and test DM before the demo

🧍

Verified by World ID

Before the SOS button is ever activated, we confirm you are a real, unique human. Proof is verified on our server — never trusted from the client.

🛡️

Fewer false reports

Human verification raises the barrier to creating many accounts that consume calling resources. It is not a guarantee against false reports or abuse.

🔒

Minimum data, by design

World ID proves human uniqueness, not your legal identity. Profile details such as name and birthday are entered by you. LIFELiNK does not receive ID photos or biometrics from this proof.

Proof of Human confirms a unique human, not a legal identity. LIFELiNK processes contact details, coarse location, event metadata and call transcripts. Read the setup guide and the README's current consent gaps before testing.
